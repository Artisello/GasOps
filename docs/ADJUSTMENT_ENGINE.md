# Lean adjustment engine

Study edition Codice di Rete XCII, chapter 9 §§5.1–5.2 (PDF pp. 404–408), chapter 18 §4.1.5 (pp. 591–593). The latter expressly states the definitive transport balances are not modified by economic adjustment. Annual baseline is the balancing session and multi-year baseline the preceding adjustment session. Version of law/applicability not certified.

MVP imports already calculated pre-tax economic amounts, with declared sign convention (DS/IO positive for user; tariff charges positive), fictional UDISA ID, processing year, date of competence, component, distinct baseline/result refs and optional communicated delta. Annual competence previous calendar year, multi-year second to fifth prior year, post-2020 only. No actual allocation formulas, regulated rate determination or computation of underlying economic amounts yet. Eight components from §5.2: DS, IO (monthly), VAR, SCOST (monthly), CVU, CVFC, CPU, CMT. CPU/CMT depend on applicable system changes; no presumption. Missing values never interpreted as zero. One immutable declared result per user/session year/day or month/component: conflicting imports reject atomically; archive export and restore, 20k rows, 100 hashes, session-only. No source authenticity proof. A later corrected release for the same key requires human review/export before replacement; do not silently append duplicate partite.

Calculate delta = new minus baseline, compare declared delta with tolerance 0.01 EUR (study threshold, not regulatory rounding). Optional consistency with previously imported participant definitive DS for annual only, same identifier/day, without changing it; not a verified baseline-reference match. Multi-year prior amount is supplied explicitly, not inferred from the first annual session; references are declared, predecessor chronology not authenticated. Keep theoretical debit and credit separated; DS/IO reverse signs use reversal/rebooking legs, zero crossings and CPU/CMT flag manual review. Do NOT claim this is the complete legal invoice grouping or generate invoices, as §4.1.5 includes specific document and applicability conditions. No automatic netting across participants, components, sessions or periods. Coverage denominators are calendar counts of each potential component, not obligations; no absent component presumed applicable or zero. All subtotals refer only to imported rows, no official full session totals. No external AI or persistence. Demo for monthly control explicitly opt-in. No new dependencies or live API.
