# Regulatory & Compliance Intelligence

Norme e documenti → Compliance. Select public PDF archive and a specific real/session case from KPI operativi → Centro operativo (not the synthetic demo). Suggested keyword themes for Operations, Balancing, Settlement and Data Quality are research aids, not asserted rules or legal obligations. Consult official Snam code, commercial notices and ARERA integrated texts/TIB. Official index links checked 18 Nov 2026; no claim of current applicability or complete source inventory. Existing bundled PDF editions are not automatically treated as current.

Choose document, physical PDF page, optional declared chapter/paragraph and a contiguous quote of 15–300 characters. The app checks the actual PDF byte hash and exact normalized page text before registering the evidence. Keyword matches and declared section are not proof of meaning. No OCR, AI calls or automatic compliance judgement. Duplicate identical references are ignored. Different case evidence versions require a new link. Review of relevance is explicitly human, with a mandatory note and time; independent of operational case decisions. Changed or missing document editions are flagged on inspection of the last indexed public archive, not live-verified. Previous links remain for export and cannot be reassessed against a different edition. No assertion of present legal force, violation or compliance; confirm edition, scope, effective dates, amendments, exceptions and approved procedure separately.

Public document references only; personal PDFs cannot be joined to an operational case. Session-local, 80 links and 20 events per link. Export JSON traceability register and see references in the operational case. Handover includes the public document links and their status as last indexed (no automatic fetch); no sensitive data, no shared persistence, no write to Snam systems. The app does not ingest new normative PDFs or automate regulatory updates. Test PDF fixtures are synthetic, not regulations.
