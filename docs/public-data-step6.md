# Step 6 — Terna Power Intelligence

Market view: Mercato e previsioni > Sistema elettrico Terna. Independent of demand models and ACER.
- 17 real labelled 2025 annual observations, rounded as published in Terna's Italian statistical summary: https://www.terna.it/it/sistema-elettrico/statistiche/pubblicazioni-statistiche (accessed Nov 2026). No reconstruction of 2024 absolute values from rounded percentages and no numerical PV generation from "over 45 TWh". Distinguish gas fuel production (gross electrical energy), total thermoelectric (net API), generation for demand and net imports.
- Optional authenticated API, documented OAuth client credentials (https://developer.terna.it/docs/read/Access_Token) and Actual Generation / Total Load at https://developer.terna.it/docs/read/apis_catalog . Credentials only server side GASOPS_TERNA_CLIENT_ID and GASOPS_TERNA_CLIENT_SECRET. No requests by default, one manual request per minute per session; token request + two GETs, no retries/redirects. No claim of a guaranteed free tier or live contract; check account terms, access and quotas. No tokens or secrets recorded in source cache or output.
- API daily analysis: 1–14 gas days through D-2; query civil dates through end day. Explicitly opt in to experimental start-of-interval interpretation (not verified in live contract), DST-aware exact UTC slots in Rome 06:00–06:00. Sum hourly GWh, 15min MW × 0.25h/1000. Missing/conflicting slots invalidate the relevant daily total, no imputation. Thermal is NOT gas-only; Self-consumption excluded from comparisons; no sum of full renewable generation or efficiency inference.
- ENTSOG three SRG aggregate mapping reused in one bounded cached request, only thermoelectric exit proxy compared to national thermal generation. Exact same gas-day pairs, Pearson >=7 variable observations, descriptive not causal or predictive. Distinct electricity/gas energy and perimeters. No inference of heat rate or participant settlement; no model input or new background jobs.
- Annual extract is not native raw bytes or linked to a historic hourly dataset. Terna API credential/live payload not available in authoring environment. Verify interval semantics with native export before operational reliance. No additional software dependency. Unit tests use synthetic fixtures, including DST and missing data.
