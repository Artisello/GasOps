# Balancing Decision Support — P0

Existing Balancing > Stato sistema, reusing the normalized Jarvis archive. No new navigation, data provider, dependency or write action. Official source context: [Jarvis public data](https://jarvis.snam.it/public-data?lang=it), [ARERA balancing](https://www.arera.it/area-operatori/bilanciamentogashtm). The target is strictly the last published SAS estimate within 90 minutes before gas-day end, not final physical imbalance, linepack sufficiency, individual DS or a market order. The imported sign is retained. No claim of short/long system probability from this proxy.

Model: existing empirical correction at comparable UTC time-to-close, last observed historical publication at/before matched cutoff (maximum one hour back), closing publication strictly before end. Prior gas days only, up to 365, >=30 training pairs, >=20 progressive out-of-training predictions for diagnostic. Estimate median of historical revisions, 10/90 quantiles, empirical sign frequencies (negative/exact zero/positive). Multiclass Brier score is sum of squared probability errors per observation; benchmark is the deterministic sign of the contemporaneous source estimate. Candidate displayed as selected only with >=5% MAE improvement versus source, >=70% observed interval coverage and Brier no worse than benchmark. These are study gates, not operational thresholds or calibration certification; selection is retrospective, independent prospective validation still needed. No probability display as selected if gate fails; source estimate remains visible. No zero imputation, no forecast in last 90 min, stale >3h blocked. Historic replay explicitly selects a pre-close vintage; later publications of target gas day excluded. UTC arithmetic handles 23/25-hour gas days. Historical publication timestamps and source provenance declared by importer, not independently authenticated.

No private inputs; no market action, valuation, trade, storage/linepack limits or formal risk signal derived from SAS. Actual closing physical/system balance and relevant operational limits require independent, authorized labels and validation. Tests use synthetic data. No claim of improved real-world performance.
