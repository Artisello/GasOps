Daily gas-day and scope (title, locational, storage), completeness explicitly declared, buys and sells positive MWh, signed pretax consideration in EUR (cost of buying and revenue of selling; can be negative when price negative), excluding fees. Complete zero-action day must be explicit; absent and partial not zero. Separate product scopes, no invented substitution. Same source scope/day reference only for title, optional, supplied by operator (do not substitute PSV/TTF or use a value that includes different products). Distinct scopes never combined. All selected month completed gas days in denominator; exclude future and active day. Atomic session replacement, exact duplicates deduplicate, conflicts reject, SHA-256 export. Partial records are shown as such, excluded from totals. No source authenticity verification.

Metrics on complete declared days only: gross buys+sells, net buy volume, observed net cost of actions = buy consideration−sales revenue, volume-weighted side prices and spread across period (may mix dates, not contemporaneous bid/ask). Comparable active title days: net cost − (buy MWh−sell MWh) × same-day comparable reference; per MWh on gross matched volume. Missing reference suppresses difference, report coverage. No causal savings estimate, no claim market trades could execute at benchmark, no action effectiveness/system security judgement, no regulated P2/P3 or ACER neutrality reconstruction. Actual neutrality requires imbalance charges and all relevant cash flows/costs, not simply action net cost. Validation uses synthetic fixtures, no real evidence of efficiency gain.
