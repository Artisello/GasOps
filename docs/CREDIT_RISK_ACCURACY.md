No generated missing prices or implicit substitutions. G is recognized effective economic value, not nominal amount; guarantee eligibility and expiries are not determined.

Patt uses 3/5/7 days from declared applicable semester regularity using irregular/due amounts for each service; non-verifiable explicitly uses 7; no automatic verification of continuity or official assessment windows. Prior calendar month full purchase-price series, arithmetic mean in EUR/kWh rounded six decimals (half-up study convention); no spot substitution. Direct point capacity × supplied applicable utilization %, distribution allocated amount, programmed foreign/storage exits by horizon day (Bizzarone/San Marino treated as direct clients where applicable by importer); zero injections. All registered PSV net sales (+)/purchases (−) supplied, including future dates outside horizon; computation includes day of snapshot PSV if declared part of Patt, no previous dates. Signed linear valuation of expected deficit and PSV at Pr, with supplied applicable Patt VAT. No implied official floor, position netting policy beyond this interpretation, validation of utilization coefficients, underlying PSV registration completeness or special participant treatment. Missing data blocks result; explicit zero lists/amounts are possible. Snapshot date before current date marked historical. SHA-256, export JSON, atomic session replacement, synthetic example, tests. No new route/dependency; no writes to external systems.

Production still requires authoritative balance, payment posting, price, PSV/capacity/allocation and collateral records, reconciled economic identities, normative interpretation and independent tie-out with official EPSu/MEPSu. No real-world performance claim.
